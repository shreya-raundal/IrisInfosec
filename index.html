REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

EU-first, and ready for the regimes you face elsewhere

We lead with the European stack because that is where obligation is sharpest, then extend the same control set to the US and India so one programme carries across your markets.

  • One control set, many regimes. Mapped once, so GDPR, DPDPA and CCPA evidence comes from the same programme.
  • Primary sources, not hearsay. We work from EUR-Lex, ENISA, NIST and ISO text, and cite the clause that applies.
  • Sector-aware. Financial services, health, technology and manufacturing each carry their own overlay, and we account for it.
